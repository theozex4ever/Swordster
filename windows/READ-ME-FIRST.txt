SWORDSTER - Thanks for playtesting!
===================================

Swordster is a small action game that's still being made. You play a hero
with three magic swords floating around you. The swords fight enemies on
their own - your job is to move around, stay alive, and use their special
attacks at the right moment.

Nothing to install. It takes about a minute to get started.


GETTING STARTED
---------------
1. Unzip the folder first.
   If you're looking at this inside a zip file, right-click the zip file
   and choose "Extract All...", then click "Extract". Open the new folder
   that appears.

2. Double-click "Swordster" (the file with the sword icon).

3. You may see a blue box saying "Windows protected your PC".
   This is normal for small games that aren't sold in a store.
   Click "More info", then click "Run anyway".
   You only need to do this the first time.

4. Wait a few seconds. The game window will open and the game starts
   right away.

To quit, just close the game window.


HOW TO PLAY
-----------
Enemies come in waves. Stay alive as long as you can.

Your swords attack nearby enemies by themselves - you don't need to press
anything for that. You focus on moving and using the special moves below.

  Move around ................ W A S D keys (or the arrow keys)
  Quick dodge ................ Spacebar
  Send all swords to a spot .. Click with the mouse
  Big special attacks ........ Q, E and R (one for each sword)
  Pause ...................... Esc
  Show / hide the help text .. H
  Play again after losing .... R

Picking up new swords:
  Some enemies carry a sword. Defeat them and the sword drops on the
  ground. Walk up to it and press 1, 2 or 3 to swap it with one of your
  swords. Each sword is fire, ice or poison - try different mixes!


IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG
-----------------------
- Nothing happens when I double-click:
  Wait 10 seconds - the first start can be slow. Make sure you unzipped
  the folder first (step 1).

- My antivirus blocked or deleted it:
  This sometimes happens with small homemade games. Let whoever sent you
  the game know - that's useful feedback too.

- The game is too small, too fast, or has no sound:
  Please tell us! Mention what kind of computer you're using.


WE'D LOVE YOUR THOUGHTS
-----------------------
There are no wrong answers - honest reactions help the most, even
"I got bored" or "I didn't get it". Just reply to the person who sent
you the game with whatever comes to mind. Some ideas:

- Was it easy to get the game running?
- Did you understand what to do? What was confusing?
- What was fun? What was annoying?
- Was it too easy or too hard?
- Did anything strange happen (freezing, glitches, crashes)?
  What were you doing when it happened?
- Would you play it again?

A picture of anything odd helps a lot. To take one, press the Windows key,
Shift and S at the same time, then drag over the screen.

Thank you!
