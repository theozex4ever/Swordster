SWORDSTER - Thanks for playtesting!
===================================

Build: @BUILD_ID@
Source commit: @SOURCE_COMMIT@

This is an early test of the new Swordster. Three spirit swords fight on
their own while you move and dash. You can fight one wave of husks, or try
the two DEV ENCOUNTERS: the CROWD (runners, brutes and a way to fling them into
each other and the wall) and the SPITTERS (green foes that keep their distance
and shoot glowing orbs; your swords can sweep the orbs away). Other sword
types, special attacks and later waves come in later builds.

Nothing to install. It takes about a minute to get started.


GETTING STARTED
---------------
1. Unzip the folder first.
   If you're looking at this inside a zip file, right-click the zip file
   and choose "Extract All...", then click "Extract". Open the new folder
   that appears. Keep Swordster.exe and Swordster.pck together in the
   same folder - the game needs both.

2. Double-click "Swordster.exe".

3. You may see a blue box saying "Windows protected your PC".
   This is normal for small games that aren't sold in a store.
   Click "More info", then click "Run anyway".
   You only need to do this the first time.

4. Wait a few seconds. The game window opens by itself.
   Click inside the window once to turn the sound on.

To quit, just close the game window.


CONTROLS
--------
  Move around ................ W A S D keys (or the arrow keys)
  Dash ....................... Space or Shift
  Pause / resume ............. P or Esc
  Start over after dying ..... R
  Next dev encounter ......... C (or the "Dev: ..." button, top right under
                               Pause). It goes normal -> crowd -> spitters ->
                               normal and RESTARTS the run each time.
  Fling nearby foes .......... F (crowd encounter only; or the Shove button)

Foes are colour-coded: red husks, small orange spiky runners, big purple
brutes and green spitters. A white dashed ring means a foe is knocked back.
In the crowd encounter, fling foes into each other or into the arena wall to
hurt them.

A spitter turns toward you, glows at its snout for half a second (a dashed
line shows where it will fire) and then spits a slow green orb. An orb hurts
(8 health) unless you are dashing or just got hurt. A striking sword that
sweeps through an orb destroys it with a green flash and a PARRY tag.

On a touch screen: drag on the left side to move; use the Dash button at
the bottom right, and the Pause, Restart and Dev buttons at the top right.


IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG
-----------------------
- Nothing happens when I double-click:
  Wait 10 seconds - the first start can be slow. Make sure you unzipped
  the folder first (step 1).

- My antivirus blocked or deleted it:
  This sometimes happens with small homemade games. Let whoever sent you
  the game know - that's useful feedback too.

- No sound: click inside the game window once, then try pausing and
  resuming. Please tell us if there is still nothing.


WE'D LOVE YOUR THOUGHTS
-----------------------
When you report anything, please include the BUILD line from the top of
this file (it is also shown in the top-left corner of the game). Some ideas:

- Was it easy to get the game running?
- Did the movement and dash feel good? Did pausing and restarting work?
- Could you tell the four kinds of foe apart? Was it clear when something
  was flung into a wall or another foe?
- In the spitter encounter, could you see a shot coming (the glow and the
  dashed line)? Could you tell a parried orb from one that hit you?
- Did you hear the sounds?
- Did anything strange happen (freezing, glitches, crashes)?
  What were you doing when it happened?
- What kind of computer are you using (Windows 10 or 11)?

A picture of anything odd helps a lot. To take one, press the Windows key,
Shift and S at the same time, then drag over the screen.

Thank you!
